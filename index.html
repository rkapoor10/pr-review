Step 6 of 10 note

The placeholder write, and the race it closes

The queued placeholder switches to the insert-only write. It cannot be reordered ahead of the enqueue, because the correlation id comes back from that call, so a slow write could previously land after the outcome and reset a finished event to queued. It also now sets executionNotAttempted(true) — a second, unstated change to the activation count.
